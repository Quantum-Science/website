QSERF's April Fools Event for 2026, which renamed the game to “BATTLE FOR DARK MATTER” and introduced a new wave of nonsense.
The event lasted for 5 days before returning to the previous version.

There is no associated version number, as it was randomised for each session.

## Public Changelog
* Fixing the DMR was getting too expensive, so we stopped. It'll be fine.
* Renamed Control Station 47 after feedback.
* The diagonal elevator broke. Maintenance should have it fixed within a few weeks.
* The crane in the DMR chamber collapsed and broke some stuff. It's fine, don't worry.
* Listened to feedback about how the fuel cells were irritating to access, and moved them.
* Bought more cranes to replace the broken one.
* CS21 accidentally got painted grey. We don't have the time to fix it right now.
* Rewired the control room. I'm not an electrician so I just hooked things up at random it'll probably work fine.
* The Third Impact happened and now all our water is red.
* Accidentally placed an anti-gravity field in Foxtrot-9. We'll remove it next week, just... figure it out.
* Someone swapped the DMR ignition button. We haven't found it yet.
* The MRC is closed. It's a public holiday, after all.
* Added Emergency Yogurt. 
* You now have a better view of Foxtrot 9 from within the control room.
* Fixed bug relating to standing on servers.
* Improved control room security.
* Pump Station Alpha now has a model showcase.
