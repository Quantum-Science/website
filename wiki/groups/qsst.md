**Quantum Structural Science Team (QSST)** is a department of [Quantum Science](/wiki/groups/qs) that houses its talented game development team, including the Quality Assurance and Weapons Analytics teams.

(derived from group description)

## Trivia
* The department is often jokingly referred to as the “Quantum Structurally Disabled Team” by its developers and the community.
* Historically, the department was originally named “Quantum Advanced Structural Science Team” under a different group, but due to a controversy in 2020, QSST was formed and eventually took its place.
