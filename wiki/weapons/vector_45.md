The **Vector-45** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is an automatic submachine gun wielded by [QDF](/wiki/groups/qdf)'s Warrant Officers and CIU's Senior Operators.
