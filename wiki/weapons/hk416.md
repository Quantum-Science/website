The **HK416** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is an Automatic/Semi-automatic rifle wielded by [QDF](/wiki/groups/qdf)'s Field Officers and QDF's Squadron Officers.
