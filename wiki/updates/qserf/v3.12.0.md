This update includes general combat balancing, new/updated weapons, and more juicy stuff!

## New Weapons
We're introducing a set of new weapons (some being variants) that should make the combat experience more diverse. We've also made some minor visual adjustments to other weapons.
* **AK-74**: An addition to the base raider armoury, replacing the current AK-47. This delivers closer performance to the newly-introduced M4, acting as a lighter version to the previous AK-47.
* **AKM**: An addition to the Tier 2 raider armoury, functioning similarly to the previous AK-47, with some slight tweaks.
* **M4**: A new variant of the M4A1 in the security armoury, being locked to burst-only while providing slightly better precision. This takes the current place of the KRISS Vector.
* **M4A1-S**: An addition to the security squadron armoury. This is a silenced version of the M4A1, providing better accuracy alongside a holographic sight.
* **RPK**: An addition to the Tier 3 raider armoury, which is a light machine gun trading versatility for firepower, with a team limit of 2.


## New Raider Armour
We're introducing a new level of armour at Tier 3 (with a limit of 2), and also moving the existing armours to their respective raider tiers, that means *everyone* can have armour now!

## Reintroducing Blast Door Controls
After being.. temporarily.. removed.. in the tartarus update over a year ago, we've finally reintroduced security's ability to remotely control blast doors across the facility from their headquarters.


## Armour Rebalancing
All combative armour across both teams have been rebalanced, including minor and significant changes.


## Weapon Rebalancing
**AA-12**:
* Reduced team limit from 3 to 2.

**Desert Eagle**:
* Reduced damage ranges by ~25%.

**FN P90**:
* Now unlocked at Junior Specialist for security.
* Reduced damage by ~8%.
* Reduced headshot damage multiplier by ~7%.

**HK416**:
* Increased equip speed by 20%.
* Increased reload speed by ~9%.

**KRISS Vector**:
* Now unlocked at Senior Field Operative.
* Reduced damage by ~14%.
* Reduced headshot damage multiplier by ~19%.

**M4A1**:
* Now unlocked at Field Operative.

**MIN-134**:
* Reduced team limit from 2 to 1.

**Raging Bull**:
* Increased spread by ~35%.
* Reduced damage ranges by ~25%.

**Remington 870**:
* Increased equip speed by ~25%.

**RPD**:
* Reduced team limit from 2 to 1.

**RPG-7**:
* Increased equip time by 50%
* Increased movement debuff by ~28%.

**Saiga-12**:
* Reduced team limit from 3 to 2.


## Miscellaneous Changes
* The phase 2 shutdown window is no longer randomised between 2 and 3 seconds, and is instead now always 3 seconds.
* Updated the Winchester's scope overlay.
* Renamed the FN FAL to the L1A1.
* Fixed a bug where the FAAS's successful phase 2 shutdown voice line never played.
* Increased the gun burst cooldown by 150%.
* Increased the minimum requirement to engage maintenance mode to 35% average fuel. ([#S0703](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1483733737782120478>))
