**Quantum Community Outreach Department (QCOD)** is a department of [Quantum Science](/wiki/groups/qs) that aims to engage its vast community by providing players with opportunities to rank up and participate in community events. It also acts as a medium to connect lower and upper ranks, ensuring that all members have a voice in the group's future.
