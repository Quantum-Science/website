**Quantum Science Energy Research Facility (QSERF)** is a [simulation video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_video_game) developed by the [Quantum Structural Science Team](/wiki/groups/qsst). It is the successor to [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2), originally built as a showcase, and was released in 2019.

## Gameplay
**QSERF** is a co-op [simulation video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_video_game) in which players assume the role of employees within the [Madison Research Center](/wiki/locations/mrc). The primary objective is to operate and maintain the [Dark Matter Reactor 01](/wiki/reactors/dmr_01), a nuclear reactor controlled through various means in which require constant observation and upkeep.

## Plot
The game takes place on the night of August 18th 1985 within the [Madison Research Center](/wiki/locations/mrc), an underground research facility that primarily houses the [Dark Matter Reactor 01](/wiki/reactors/dmr_01) prototype nuclear reactor.

## Development
### QSML2 origins
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/games/qsml2/qsml2_updated.jpg
|text=QSML2's Updated DMR Control Room
}}
Following the late development of [QSML3v3](/wiki/games/qsml3) in early 2019, the team was exhausted after complications with the game's performance, and were demotivated to continue working on the project. Following this, JustSimplyWild, iXev, and Lectoric went on to experiment with the predecessor [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2) by retrofitting it with what they learned from working on [QSML3v3](/wiki/games/qsml3) and other projects, through which went on to receive graphical improvements with the team's newly improved skills.

One night, Wild decided to return to an idea originally conceived during the development of [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2) in 2017, based on the thought of the facility having an "abandoned" segment. Following this, the team went through previous versions of the game to uncover what would become the older segments of the facility, and went on to renovate the improved map with these new changes. During this process, Wild built a control room within one of the abandoned areas overlooking a large pit with a vault door, which iXev suggested the idea of it being the facility's topside entrance, with an elevator leading down through the vault door.

This would later become known as the Topside Tartarus Zone alongside the Tartarus Seal. After demonstrating the area to friends, it quickly received praise, and iXev suggested the original idea of developing a prequel game primarily dedicated to the aesthetic of Aperture Science Innovators from [Portal 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portal_2). In April 2019, the team went on to begin development of what would become known as the **QS Energy Research Facility**.

### Early development
The project went on to receive several different iterations and alterations, with the original idea in mind of designing the facility to be small and "quick to the point".
