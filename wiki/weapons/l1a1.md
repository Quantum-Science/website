The **L1A1** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/qserf_weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/qserf). It is a semi-automatic rifle wielded by Tier 2 Raiders, [QDF](/wiki/qdf)'s Senior Specialists, and CIU's Operators.

Added in the [May 2026 Combat Update](/wiki/qserf_3.12.0), it was renamed from the FN FAL to align with the weapon's in-game lack of an automatic firing mode for balancing reasons, the two versions are functionally and visually identical.

## Statistics
The weapon is intended for long-range usage, and has an inversed damage falloff.
