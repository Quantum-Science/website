QSERF v3.6.8 is the eighth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which fixes various issues.

## Changes
* Disabled audio collisions for all PA System Emitters.
* Reverted melee items being removed from the spawning loadouts of combative teams, as this was accidentally included in the last patch.

## Fixes
* Fixed a bug where the default cursor would show in third person while holding a weapon.
* Fixed the scaling of certain decorative keycard readers.
* Fixed a bug where the camera would be positioned above your avatar. ([#0502](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1397239391034020021>))
* Fixed a bug where generator fuel storage could become permanently unusable. ([#0442](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1378142251288367225>))
* Fixed a bug where certain weapons were unintentionally unrestricted from the give command. ([#0507](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1397854710983163944>))
* Fixed a bug where certain event weapons were unintentionally restricted from the give command.
