QSERF v3.6.18 is the eighteenth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which fixes several issues.

## Fixes
* Temporarily changed `Workspace.ModelStreamingBehavior` to `Legacy` in order to resolve the issues relating to avatars.
* MGKS, the combat system, should no longer fail to load.
* Disabled the "October 8th Character Debugger".
* You might notice some doors or other gameplay features will very rarely fail, this is a result of the active bug we mentioned earlier and we're waiting on Roblox to provide a timeline for resolve.
