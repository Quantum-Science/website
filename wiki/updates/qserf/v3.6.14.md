QSERF v3.6.14 is the fourteenth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which fixes various issues.

## Additions
* Added more spooky decorations (and also fixed some floating pumpkins).

## Changes
* Foxtrot-9's reactor rod buttons no longer use transparency. ([#0613](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1422980579078570094>))

## Fixes
* Fixed a bug where the P.A. System was 2x louder than it should be.
* Fixed parts of the DMRCR wall trim that weren't updated.
* Fixed the DMR interior floor missing a weld constraint. ([#0612](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1422966218251436113>))
* Fixed event decoration setting. ([#0615](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1423150572273664021>))
* Fixed a bug where holding right click in first person while holstering a weapon would reduce your sensitivity. ([#0610](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1422956991269048512>))
* Improved the security checks for world interactions. ([#0168](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1340864495983005767>), [#0596](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1417990739350130730>))
