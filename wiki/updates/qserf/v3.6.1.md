QSERF v3.6.1 is the first patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), fixing various issues.

## Changes
* Adjusted generator idle rolloff.

## Fixes
* Fixed collisions of a wall outside the DMRCR Records Room. (closes [#0249](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1349132285143941120>))
* Fixed a bug where the raider hacking device could trigger the warhead's admin override. (closes [#0251](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1349217755659763814>))
* Fixed payouts script being utterly silly. (closes [#0243](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348821657900286093>))
* Fixed a bug where the cursor would disappear if you died while holding a weapon. (closes [#0260](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1349545940255637624>))
* Fixed a bug where the phase 1 music didn't stop during the `???` meltdown event
* Fixed a server-side memory leak during phase 1 shutdown attempt.
* Fixed issues with camera sensitivity. (closes [#0233](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348732642811445368>))
* Fixed clothing dummy positions of `armour_giver_security_tier_3` & `armour_giver_security_tier_4`.
* Fixed issues with command permissions for private servers (closes [#0230](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348723632851128361>))
* Fixed a typo on the note outside of the Mail Office. (closes [#0239](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348759217707942038>))
* Fixed the `Bauble Gernade`, `Bat Gernade`, and `Dynamite Soap`. (closes [#0240](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348760567019606116>))
* Fixed decals on generator models.
* Improved how character ground search distance is calculated. (closes [#0149](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1337853019215233044>))
* Removed duplicate instance of the crane above Foxtrot-9. (closes [#0234](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1348737473320058900>))
* Adjusted collision mesh for the wall surrounding a door outside the shaft elevator.
* Alarm `err_x2` is now stopped during the `???` meltdown event.
