The **Auto Assault-12 (AA-12)** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is an Automatic shotgun wielded by [QDF](/wiki/groups/qdf)'s Senior Specialists and SEU's Operators.
