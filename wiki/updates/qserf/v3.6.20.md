QSERF v3.6.20 is the twentieth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), ending [Halloween 2025](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.13) and fixing various issues.

## Changes
* The halloween event is over! The Jack-o'-lantern and spook emote will return next year.
* Undisclosed security improvements.

## Fixes
* Fixed a bug where the DMR's exterior cables were missing.
* Fixed a bug where the DMR's exterior cables wouldn't melt off during meltdown.
