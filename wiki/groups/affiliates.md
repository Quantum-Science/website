title: Affiliates 
---

In an effort to give back to the community we've helped foster, Quantum Science has a number of affiliated groups, which we wholeheartedly endorse you to join and give some of your time to! Below is a list of our current affiliates, which will be periodically updated with new groups, so keep an eye out for updates.<br/>

**How do I become affiliated with QS?** Unfortunately we don't currently have plans to open this program to the wider community, it is restricted to groups we have good relations with and find their communities to be unique, this may be updated later, so once again, keep an eye out.

## Athena Computer, Inc.
A group that takes after both Innovation and Portal in the best ways. These guys have some really creative ideas for Sci-Fi facilities, as well as computer science in general.
* ["Athena Computer, Inc."](https://discord.gg/zws32EsgZr) — Discord.

## Elevatorz Station 
The community server for Elevatorz' YouTube channel, they have been a long-time friend of QS, and we love the Roblox Sci-Fi commentary videos he makes. 
* ["Elevatorz Station"](https://discord.gg/AxvM8ab) — Discord.
* ["elevatorz89"](https://www.youtube.com/@elevatorz89) — YouTube.

## Astral Industries
Astral Industries is a development studio and event producer which specializes in the sci-fi genre. You may recognize them from their annual convention, Astral Central, which brings together a large amount of groups to all celebrate and showcase their work in one place.
* ["Astral Industries"](https://discord.gg/dtshB6DmsW) — Discord.

## Bunker Bravo Interactive
Bunker Bravo Interactive is a game publisher founded on the values of immersive storytelling and dynamic gameplay; similar to the subsidiary Nuclear Engineering Co.; they pride themselves on being some of the best of the best it comes to putting out enjoyable experiences for all.<br/>
They dip their toes into many different genres, and host a few different studio under their wings to combine development prowess and streamline production.
* ["Bunker Bravo Interactive LLC"](https://discord.gg/s8acKqr79t) — Discord.

## Innovation Inc. Thermal Power Plant
Innovation Inc. Thermal Power Plant is an official fan game under Innovation Inc., created by AnimeRiceFarmer and his development team. Explore a unique science facility situated within a volcano, featuring reactor-focused gameplay, large-scale events, and much more. Join the server to stay up to date with the latest news, updates, and community events.
* ["Innovation Inc. Thermal Power Plant"](https://discord.gg/dcDbYzf) — Discord.
