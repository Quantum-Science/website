QSERF v3.6.7 is the seventh patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which rebalances combat.

## Changes
* The following weapons have had their statistics updated:
  * `G-17`, `G-18`, `G-18 Duals`, `R870`
* Removed the teleport bricks in the DMRCR security bypass room.
* Updated combat items to reflect recent changes to QDF Squadron loadouts.
