The **AK-74** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is an Automatic/Semi-automatic rifle wielded by Tier 1 Raiders.

Added in the [May 2026 Combat Update](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.12.0), it serves as the replacement for the now-removed AK-47, albeit with closer performance to the [M4](/wiki/weapons/m4).
