The **AK-47** was a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It was an automatic assault rifle wielded by Tier 1 Raiders.

Removed in the [May 2026 Combat Update](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.12.0), it was replaced by the newly-introduced [AK-74](/wiki/weapons/ak_74).
