## Synopsis
The game takes place in the early 2000s and is located at Gore Creek, Colorado, the facility has some wear but is still fully operational, providing energy to the surrounding area.

## Plot
Following the QSERF disaster in 1985 and soon after the nuclear ban, QSCorp branched out into other forms of energy generation in order to prevent the company collapsing (quite a while after QSERF ill have to look this up) and served the company up until the late 2000s. Employees have to manage all parts of the plant, water levels, energy generation, and maintenance, the site is also prone to severe storms and flooding. 

## Gameplay
DHP aims to be a realistic simulation of a regular hydroelectric power plant, as 1:1 as we can get it while also making gameplay fun, planning for this hasn't been set in stone, so gameplay will not be disclosed here just yet.

## Development
DHP was announced in early 2022 by JustSimplyWild, and was based on the Hoover Dam which he had visited a few months prior, since then it hadn't had much work done to it, except for a brief period in early 2024 when its layout was redone and a more concrete plan was set in place for the whole games design, which involved moving away from copying Hoover Dam.<br/>
As of writing, not much more has been done to this project and it is still on hold for the foreseeable future.
