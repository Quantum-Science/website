QSERF v3.7.1 is the first patch following [v3.7.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.7.0), fixing various issues.

## Additions
* Added a uniform giver in raider spawn for the unofficial [Kleiner Oil Group](<https://www.roblox.com/communities/8592261>).
* Added christmas uniforms for security operatives.
* Added back the weapon models to the RPD & RPG-7 weapon cases in raider spawn.

## Changes
* Adjusted the smoothness of certain portions of snow terrain very slightly.
* Removed two office chairs that were out of bounds.
* The pass requirements for the raider arsenal are now more clear.

## Fixes
* Fixed a bug where the "Goals" note in raider spawn had z-indexing issues.
* Fixed a bug where you could sit in Dr. Weldman's not very usable office chair. ([#0475](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1387956112078278686>))
* Fixed a bug where the Festive Speed Coil wasn't given to owners of the Speed Coil Pass.
* Fixed a bug where admins were unable to force emotes, and the minimum rank is now UMO.
* Fixed the brazil & detroit "emotes". ([#0280](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1350587349595066419>))
* Fixed a few building errors relating to the christmas decorations. ([#0711](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1442186692646932490>), [#0713](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1442309500718547046>))
* Fixed a dozen or so office chairs that were unintentionally unusable. ([#0679](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1438065582703378494>))
