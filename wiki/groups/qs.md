**Quantum Science (QS)** is an indie game development studio that specializes in the sci-fi realm.<br/>

Founded by JustSimplyWild (TheWildDeveloper) and seaofbree(BmanGames) on the 6th of September 2014, Quantum Science has strived to advance the Roblox Sci-Fi communities standards with every release. The group has had multiple owners over the years, including JustSimplyWild, seaofbree, iXev, EnigmaTech and now lavafactory. <br/>

At the time of writing, Quantum has 484k+ members and steadily rising.
