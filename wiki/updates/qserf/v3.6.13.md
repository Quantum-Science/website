QSERF v3.6.13 is the thirteenth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which introduced the year's halloween event.

## Additions
* Added seasonal halloween decorations (yes we reused them again).
* Added new name colours—as always, you'll have to discover these yourself!
* Added an invisible barrier to the ceiling of the DMR Control Room to prevent abuse of gaps.
* Added a goto-location for the Foxtrot-9 Control Room.
* The Jack-o'-lantern and spook emote have returned to the shop!

## Changes
* All NPCs now use atomic streaming.
* Improved the Foxtrot-9 Controls—including a better diagram, screens, buttons, and more!
* Improved the wall trim and fixed building errors in the DMR Control Room.
* Lower Management Office can now join the Administration team.
* Restricted items are now limited to Upper Management Office+.
* Updated text chat tags to align with recent rank changes.

## Fixes
* WE DID IT... WE DID IT... or technically i did- THE DMR NO LONGER KILLS YOUR FRAMERATE WHEN IT MOVES!!!
  * Applied awesome cool magic to massively reduce the frame times during DMR movement, following this—combatives will now also see movement!
  * The DMR interior now unloads during startup if no one is inside.
  * The DMR vault door can now be manually closed during startup.
  * Optimised the DMR interior a little.
* Fixed the lighting within the Foxtrot-9 Control Room.
* Fixed a bug where zooming in with a gun would unintentionally reduce your movement speed if first person zooming was enabled.
* Fixed a bug where zooming could get stuck while equipping a non-aimable weapon if first person zooming was enabled.
* Fixed a bug where the Black Hole had stage fright and sometimes wouldn't appear. ([#0190](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1344154839042490469>))
* Disabled automatic network ownership on the Tartarus Fans to hopefully fix a bug where they would sometimes glitch out of place. ([#0525](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1399871205464473672>))
