**Quantum Science Multipurpose Laboratories 1 (QSML1)** was a [simulation video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_video_game) developed in 2016 by JustSimplyWild and SeaOfBreeze. In January of 2017, an update released in which the core was scripted to be functional with a meltdown sequence, and it boosted the Quantum group past the 100 members mark, gaining it an active player base. QSML1 was Quantum's first game in which you could cold-start the reactor, which went on to be a popular feature of our games. The core systems script was based on a modified version of BrickExpress's "Gate" system which was popular at the time, being the oldest known open-source core systems script. BrickExpress, also known as Gin0, is a founding member of the Quantum Science group. The game included a physics lab with a giant shredder you could throw objects into, and a tank combat arena. Notably, the entrance to the facility featured 15 flights of stairs, which could be avoided through a teleporter gained access to by joining the group. QSML1 was discontinued after being succeeded by [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2)..
