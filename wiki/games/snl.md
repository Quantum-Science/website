**Sitka National Laboratory (SNL)** is an upcoming game developed by the [Quantum Structural Science Team](/wiki).
It is the successor to [QSML3](/wiki/games/qsml3), and is set in 2042 after the events of [QSERF](/wiki/games/snl).
