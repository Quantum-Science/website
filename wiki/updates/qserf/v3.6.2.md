QSERF v3.6.2 is the second patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), fixing various issues and finishing cut content.

Due to unexpected issues after release, the patch was reverted and subsequently re-released as [v3.6.2: Deluxe](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.2-deluxe) for April Fools.

## Additions
* Implemented the annunciators inside the Topside Tartarus Control Room!
* Implemented a new backend for managing player teams, this also resolves the current layout bug.
* Implemented a new animation system backend for doors, tape drives, and the player character.
* If a Phase 1 Shutdown cannot proceed due to a random event, such as a catalyser failure or ???, the chance of Phase 2's Shutdown will increase to 75%. (Suggestion: #0109)
* You can now reset your user data once again.
* Reskinned Vector-45 to Dark Blue.
