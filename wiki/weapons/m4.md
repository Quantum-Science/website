The **M4** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/qserf_weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/qserf). It is a semi-automatic rifle wielded by [QDF](/wiki/qdf)'s Junior Field Operatives.

Added in the [May 2026 Combat Update](/wiki/qserf_3.12.0), it serves as a variant of the [M4A1](/wiki/m4a1) that provides improved precision, while being locked to the burst fire mode.
