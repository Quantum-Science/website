**Quantum Science Multipurpose Laboratories 2 (QSML2)** was a [simulation video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_video_game) developed in 2017 by the [Quantum Structural Science Team](/wiki/groups/qsst), being the first group-developed project under Quantum. It was built by Wild and iXev, scripted by AdjacentPhoenix, SeaOfBreeze, Systemicist, and Wild. QSML2 was the first to introduce the [Dark Matter Reactor Concept](/wiki/idk/dmr_01). The shape largely inspired by Fan Zhang's Engine Room concept art wasn't quite nailed down, however it had a unique early prototype vibe we leaned into by covering the structure in cables, etc. thus becoming the first iteration of the DMR. QSML2 laid out features such as logistics, map destruction, emergency shutdown endings, and various aspects of the DMR's gameplay. <br/>

It was the first game to feature Facility Automated Announcement voice acting from DTrainsMan123, known for his voice in QSERF. The occasional game asset from this period of time can still be found in modern QSERF. During the failure route of the emergency shutdown ending, there was an animation of the gravity laser failing, dropping the DMR onto it's pedestal, where it appears to sit before toppling over, ripping the connecting cables as it falls into the pit below, exploding once it hits the bottom.<br/>

QSML2 was discontinued in 2019 in favour of [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf), and can now be found under the [Quantum Archival Department](/wiki/groups/qad).
