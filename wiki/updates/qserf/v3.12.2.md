This update includes general combat balancing, new/updated weapons, and more juicy stuff!

## Changelog
* Rebalanced armour & weapons following community feedback and internal testing, full details in the thread below.
* The blast door controls can now be used by Squadron Officers in the Control & Intervention Unit.
* The Glock-18 Duals & M4A1-S no longer have a muzzle flash.
* Fixed a bug where multiple weapons of the same class could be equipped. ([#R1006](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1511116495378452653>))
* Fixed a bug where security could rarely spawn out of bounds.
* Fixed a bug where the Teleportation Device & WCS Device were rarely broken. ([#R0969](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1506036973561712791>))
* Fixed various issues with the security cameras. ([#R0851](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1476339474647748669>), [#R0979](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1509231681821999104>))
* Fixed the M4A1 & M4A1-S sounds not being as intended.

## Armour Rebalancing
**Raider Tier 3**:
* Decreased bullet resistance by 10%.
* Decreased explosive resistance by 10%.

**Security Tier 3**:
* 🆕  Now unlocked at Senior Operative for the Suppression & Enforcement Unit.

**Security Tier 4**:
* Increased bullet resistance by ~3%.
* Increased explosive resistance by ~3%.

## Weapon Rebalancing
**AK-74**:
* Increased maximum damage by ~5%.

**AKM**:
* Increased accuracy by 9%.
* Increased sight accuracy by 15%.
* Increased recoil by ~11%.

**FN P90**:
* Now unlocked at Senior Field Operative for Security.

**Frag Grenade**:
* Now unlocked at Junior Specialist for Security.
* 🆕  Now unlocked at Junior Operative for Security Squadrons.

**Glock-18**:
* Increased maximum damage by 17%.

**Glock-18 Duals**:
* Now unlocked at Senior Officer for Security.

**KRISS Vector**:
* Now unlocked at Field Operative.
* Increased maximum damage by 17%.

**L1A1**:
* Now unlocked at Senior Specialist for Security.

**MIN-134**:
* Now unlocked at Specialist for Security.

**Riot Shield**:
* Now unlocked at Operative for the Suppression & Enforcement Unit.

**RPD**:
* Increased reload speed by 17%.

**RPK**:
* Decreased dropoff range by ~23%.
* Increased reload speed by 25%.

**Winchester M70**:
* Now unlocked at Senior Operative for the Control & Intervention Unit.
