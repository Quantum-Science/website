It's that time of the year again—the festive season is upon us and we're here to spread the cheer!

## Changelog
* Reintroduced Christmas decorations and the very-much-red hazmat suit for a limited time!
* Added a uniform giver in raider spawn for the unofficial [Meridian Assault Squadron](<https://www.roblox.com/communities/106590178/Meridian-Assault-Squadron>).
* The navigation logic for hazmat suit givers are now independent from each other.
* Reduced the lighting brightness in raider spawn to negate the intense bloom on the rules board.
