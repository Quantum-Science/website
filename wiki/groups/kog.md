**Kleiner Oil Group or KOG** is a canonical group of oil companies in the Quantum Universe, which hires mercenaries to wreak havoc in Quantum Sciences facilities, in attempts to disprove the DMR's efficiency, safety and overall superiority to non renewable forms of energy.<br/>
KOG continues to be a rival to QDF in SNL, with the oil and gas industry becoming increasingly smaller.<br/>

KOG was created in late 2020 by imskyyc, under the name **The Quantum Insurgency or TQI**, it was one of the first unoffical raider groups that QS ever had. KOG was created not long after actually within QS, but never gained much traction, since then TQI and KOG have merged and is now a subdepartment of [QCOD](/wiki/groups/qcod).
