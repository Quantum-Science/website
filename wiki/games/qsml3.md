**Quantum Science Multipurpose Laboratories 3 (QSML3)** was a string of games starting in 2018, with QSML3 Pre Alpha and went on to recieve 7 different iterations over the years that followed.<br/>
All of these versions are owned and have at some point be developed by [Quantum Structural Science Team](/wiki/groups/qsst).<br/>
QSML3 in lore is the home of the 2nd Generation Dark Matter Reactor and goes on to be commercially available to the world, it is regarded as the safest nuclear fusion reactor.

## QS Multipurpose Laboratories 3 Pre-Alpha (V1)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/3PreAlpha.png
|text=QSML3v1's DMR Control Room
}}

QSML3 Pre-Alpha was made by Wild, iXev, Bree, and Sbeve in Early 2018 after [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2). It did originally have the QSML2 DMR or the DMR1 in the game, but was soon replaced by the DMR2 based on concept art from Fan Zhang on ArtStation, there are only videos of this version on the Quantum Science YouTube Channel, with an archive under the QASST group, as of writing this archive currently isnt public.

## QS Multipurpose Laboratories 3 Alpha (V2)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/3Alpha.png
|text=QSML3v2's DMR Control Room
}}

QSML3 Alpha was made after Pre-Alpha and was once again built by Wild, iXev, Bree, and Sbeve later on in 2018, this iterated on the DMR2 and the game as a whole, introducing more detail throughout the facility, and more complex DMR controls. This version is uncopylocked and free to use by the community, as of writing, you may have to ID verify to download this game and use the website instead of the roblox app.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## Pinewood Builders Multipurpose Research Facility (V3)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/PBMRF.png
|text=PBMRF's DMR Control Room
}}

PBMRF was created when Wild joined PB's XYLEM team after leaving QS in mid 2018, this was developed up until the end of 2018 when the project was scrapped, and Wild left PB and went back to QS. This version remade almost everything from QSML3 Alpha, it most notably included a rebuilt DMR2 and chamber, while also adding many functional details to the game. This version can be found under PB's historacle archive under the PBMRF group, and is not uncopylocked and therefore may not be used by the community.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## QS Multipurpose Laboratories Version 3 Showcase (V4)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/3Showcase.png
|text=QSML3v4's DMR Control Room
}}

Version 3 Showcase was built after PBMRF and was in fact built on top of PBMRF as there are still parts of PBMRF under the chamber, this was built by (currently dont know who worked on it properly, wild definitely). It introduced a higher amount of detail than previously found in QS games at the time, and included some areas now found in QSERF. This version also included a different startup to previous QSML games, which is quite possibly one of the best sounding when paired with the startup soundtrack. This version can be found under the QAD group, and is not uncopylocked and therefore may not be used by the community.
Eventually this version was scrapped due to performance issues and low motivation.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## Johnson National Laboratory V1 (V5)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/JNLV1.png
|text=JNLv1's Facility Topside
}}

This was a weird in between project that separates QSML3 Showcase and JNL and was built by Wild in 2019, it included the QSERF topside building along with some newer looking hallways not found in other QSML3 versions.
This version has not been made public, and likely won't be made into an archive due to how little there is to see in this version.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>
 
## Johnson National Laboratory V2 (V6)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/JNLV2.png
|text=JNLv2's DMR Control Room
}}

Built in Early 2020 by Wild and NSDoppler, under the group North Star Energy which was quickly scrapped when Wild rejoined QS (We are unsure of the exact timeline of events here). This version included a brand new map and brand new DMR2 model and controls, it unfortuantely didn't get very far as it was scrapped by Wild in mid 2020 after a falling out with staff relating to the project and overall not liking the projects direction.
As of writing this version currently isnt public or under any official archive, it is also not uncopylocked and therefore may not be used by the community.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## Sitka National Laboratory (V7)
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/snl.png
|text=SNL's Inner DMR Chamber
}}

{{main|/wiki/games/snl|Sitka National Laboratory}}

**Sitka National Laboratory (SNL)** was announced in late 2020 and was being built by Wild, but has since been taken over by a brand new development team. SNL has had a bumpy development process since it was announced and has been through many iterations already, with the original building being scrapped, but the ideas kept and iterated on, as of writing we finally have a layout set in stone and have begun building it out, there is no release date for this project yet but we are working on it almost daily at this point.
<div style="clear: both;"></div>

## QS Multipurpose Laboratories³ V3
{{infobox
|image=asset/image/wiki/game_screenshots/3V3.png
|text=QSML³ V3's DMR Control Room
}}

V3 started off as a fan project built by imskyyc in 2020 and several other developers (ill add them in later) and significantly overhauled parts of the Showcase map, it included a fully functioning DMR2 with a meltdown which most previous versions didn't have, this version eventually included a rebuilt DMR2 and all surrounding equiment, sadly this version didn't end up being released as the project was shut down in Mid 2021. This version can be found on the QASST group, and is not uncopylocked and therefore may not be used by the community.
