The **Remington 870 (R870)** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is a Pump-action shotgun wielded by Tier 1 Raiders and [QDF](/wiki/groups/qdf)'s Junior Field Operatives.
