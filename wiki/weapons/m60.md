The **M60** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/games/qserf/weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/games/qserf). It is an Automatic machine gun wielded by [QDF](/wiki/groups/qdf)'s Senior Field Officers and SEU's Senior Operators.
