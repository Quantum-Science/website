QSERF v3.5.0 is the annual christmas update for 2025, titled the “Tartarus Update”.

Note: This page is largely incomplete, is missing many changes, and includes improperly formatted items.

## Additions
* Added christmas decorations.
* Added new Posters
* Added new icon to the Christmas Latte
* Added toxic gases to the bottom of the R.O Cavern
* New Tartarus Zone & Sector A 
* New Raider Spawn
  * This new spawn is in a different location, and the other spawn locations have been **removed**.
* New Maintenance Elevator Shaft
* Players can now walk around on moving elevators.
* implemented the emergency pressure release system (temperature has to be over 3200 for it to be work) with a 2 minute cooldown for testing, when activated shutters will close and the dmr chamber will become irradiated for a period as the temperature drops by 750k
* new tape drive animations! they also get a little angry when FAAS works hard during the meltdown now
* dome event head settings are now present

## Changes
* Chairs now have an interaction prompt, instead of being touch-based.
* Combative armour now uses a damage-reduction system, instead of adding health
* Overhauled most doors, plus a new bonus door!
* Overhauled Keycard Readers & Hacking System
* Overhauled Anti-gravity, is more fun to control
* Overhauled the riot shield
* Improved audio balancing & effects
* Improved Cargo Bay Storage
* Improved Outfit Givers, can now switch between & unequip outfits
* Most audio is now muted during endings
* Refreshed & added new props
* Refreshed Sublevel-1 & Sublevel-2
* Sector B is no longer split between Sector A and B.
* you now uncrouch upon jumping/falling
* after a successful phase 2 shutdown the red fog should now no longer remain until you go back to the cr
* use of vents after resetting/venturing away from the vent no longer works
* blackout occurs AFTER 20 second countdown is finished during startup instead of at the beginning
* the backend api has now been rewritten in rust! yay

## Fixes
* bug where foxtrot 9 would "duplicate" its functions after regenning (i.e the sync dial glitching around) fixed
* nightshift temperature displays no longer go to like -99f
* fixed (one) warhead brick
* no more "you are doing good on nightshift" halfway through if meltdown/warhead is going on
* dmr and tartarus on tween ignorelist so no matter your distance theyll render now
* relief valves can cause high radiation in the chamber!
* changed it so nightshift cannot end if the warhead is primed, this resets when the warhead is disengaged
* diagonal elevator bricking when you press the green instead of red button
* ability to cause a nightshift end with the warhead on anything but ready and inactive is gone
* npc spawning effects showing up when you have npc effects disabled is fixed
* more attempts to stop emote spamming
* the button for the f9 vault door is now functional
* major weapon system changes (optimisations, weapon falloff (including damage voiding and wawa work, rpg now fires from the gun instead of your torso)
* FIX TO PEOPLE ANCHORING THEMSELVES by unequipping the tp watch and teleporting??
* stop warhead arming keys showing up when you switch teams
* event items will no longer be given out randomly (plus we now use the new fancy badge fetching function)
* the issue with miniguns where the revving sound would stay after being unequipped has been fixed
* fixed an error with the dmr door blowing off in metldown
* fixed the armored vault sometimes desyncing
