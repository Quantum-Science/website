**Quantum Science Multipurpose Laboratories 1 (QSML1)** was a [simulation video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_video_game) developed by JustSimplyWild.
It was discontinued in 2017, and succeeded by [QSML2](/wiki/games/qsml2).
