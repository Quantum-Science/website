**Quantum Broadcasting Corporation (QBC)** is a subdepartment of the [Quantum Structural Science Team](/wiki/groups/qsst) that produces media content for the group, for entertainment or promotional purposes. The content created by QBC can vary wildly, from videos to literature to music and anything in-between. This is a passion-driven department, composed of motivated and creative individuals who would like to make their ideas a reality for others to enjoy.<br/>
If you would like to view this content, you can do so on the Quantum Science YouTube channel linked below.<br/>

You can join QBC through applications posted in QSP, these are only opened when more staff are required, they hire a range of staff, such as writers, set builders, and video editing staff.
