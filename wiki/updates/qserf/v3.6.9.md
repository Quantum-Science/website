QSERF v3.6.9 is the ninth patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which fixes various issues.

## Changes
* Building Tools (F3X) is now available to admins via the give command.
* Removed the loading screen fact referencing Chernobyl due to moderation concerns.

## Fixes
* Fixed QDF squadron permission issues relating to the Vector-45 and Juggernaut Armour.
* Made improvements to the bugged avatar ground offset correction process.
* Removed broken tooltips from decorative keycard readers that were accidentally included in the last patch.
* Partially rewrote the client lighting effects system, fixes a common bug where portions of the facility would become permanently dark, regardless of the lights being on.
* The televisions have been properly placed by a certified television-placer-person, and in turn are no longer upside down.
* Fixed a bug where the Jetpack could not determine the ground material which resulted in it thinking you were always mid-air, this also fixes a visual issue where the hover effects were never present.
