The **RPG-7** is a type of [Weapon](/wiki/qserf_weapons) in [QSERF](/wiki/qserf). It is a hand-held rocket launcher wielded by Tier 4 Raiders, [QDF](/wiki/qdf)'s Senior Field Officers, and QDF's Senior Squadron Officers.

## Statistics
Technically the explosive damage leads up to infinity with a theoretical perfect hit, but the intended maximum is 600 damage at a distance of 1 stud.
