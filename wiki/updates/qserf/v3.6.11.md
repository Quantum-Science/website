QSERF v3.6.11 is the eleventh patch following [v3.6.0](/wiki/updates/qserf/v3.6.0), which fixes various issues.

## Changes
* Buttons logs are now cleared when the server regenerates. ([#0563](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1407164134180519937>))
* Updated the Raider Agreement to clarify future changes and your acknowledgement.
  * we also have the guidelines on the devforum now! https://devforum.roblox.com/t/3892850

## Fixes
* Fixed a bug where game emotes were unusable, you can also use them with `/emote` now. ([#0568](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1408090717695250442>))
* Fixed a bug where the Janitor Bucket could not be placed down. Janitorial staff rejoice! ([#0353](<https://discord.com/channels/939956361977020449/1357388572029620374>))
* Made sure all currently used animations have permission to be used by the experience.
